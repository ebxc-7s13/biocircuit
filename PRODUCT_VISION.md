# BioCircuit product vision

BioCircuit makes useful biomedical engineering work easier to find and build together. It prioritizes clear technical questions, evidence-backed answers, honest project logs and scholarly records with provenance.

The visual identity is a living research instrument: warm paper and ink surfaces, spectral green/blue/amber/violet accents, editorial scientific typography and restrained microscopy-inspired movement. Desktop research panels advance left across a horizontal canvas with an accessible side rail; mobile remains a vertical reading experience.

Membership is a process: managed phone authentication, minimal private academic application and an authorized reviewer decision. Current undergraduate/postgraduate/doctoral pathways and result-awaiting applicants can apply. Optional grades are private and never affect discovery or reputation.

Reputation records accepted answer events, deduplicates awards and invalidates removed contributions. Research interests, methods, source quality, freshness and diversity guide explainable discovery; institution prestige, grades and reaction volume do not.

The canonical specification remains MASTER_IMPLEMENTATION_PROMPT.md. Implementation evidence and remaining external dependencies are maintained in docs/IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md and docs/BUILD_REPORT.md. Original vision text is preserved in docs/baseline/PRODUCT_VISION.md.txt.
